## Part A — Southwest Airlines Flight Delay Analysis

As part of the business context, I analyzed U.S. flight data over a 3‑month period to help Southwest Airlines leadership understand delay patterns and compare their performance to other carriers.

### Key Insights

- **Mesa Airlines** had the highest percentage of late flights: **78.37%**  
- **Flights originating in Wisconsin (WI)** had an average arrival delay of **58.64 minutes**  
- Airlines with a **lower average arrival delay than Southwest** included:  
  **Hawaiian Airlines, Aloha Airlines, Delta Airlines**

### Skills Demonstrated

- Calculated fields for delay classification  
- Aggregation and filtering  
- Comparative performance analysis  
- Dashboard design for operational insights

## Part B — Video Game Sales Analysis

In this scenario, I acted as a market analyst for a major video game publisher and explored global sales trends across platforms, genres, and publishers.

### Key Insights

- **Global sales in 2008:** **678.9 million**  
- **Nintendo platform with the highest fighting‑genre sales:** **Wii**  
- **Number of games published by Ubisoft:** **917**

### Skills Demonstrated

- Multi‑chart dashboard creation (line chart + stacked bar chart)  
- Genre and platform filtering  
- Publisher‑level aggregation  
- Sorting and highlight tables
